What does the Fourier transform look like?

Gate 1 · 72 development items · 12 per family · no model evaluations

44 simulator checks passed. Numerical option distinctness passed for all 72 items. This is a physics and presentation review, not a benchmark result.

Intensity display: g=log10(1+999 I/Imax)/3; each full plane is normalized to its own peak before cropping. Source and mask panels show linear amplitude transmission (white=1). Source/output field: 192 units across; frequency field: [-0.125,0.125) cycles/unit on both axes. Coordinates: x right, y down. All panels in the same plane share a scale.

For complementary screens, DC is hidden and only native Fourier samples are displayed; their non-DC maximum sets the brightness. The equality does not hold at arbitrary frequencies between those samples. The 4f relay's overall inversion is removed by coordinate registration.

Review the answer hidden under each card, the mask orientation, field of view, distractor plausibility and visibility. Log and linear cards use identical geometry, answer order and sampling. Numerical distinctness is not evidence that a model can distinguish the options.
